How to Play

Game Objective

Be the first player to get rid of all your cards by matching colors or numbers with the card on the discard pile.

Getting Started

1. Each player starts with 7 cards.

2. The top card of the draw pile is flipped to start the discard pile.

3. Players take turns clockwise, starting with you.

4. On your turn, you must play a card that matches either the color or number of the top card on the discard pile.

Card Types

Number Cards (0-9): Match by color or number
Skip Card: Next player loses their turn
Reverse Card: Changes direction of play
Draw Two Card: Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn
Wild Card: Play on any card and choose the next color
Wild Draw Four: Next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn

Special Rules

  • When you have only one card left, you must click the "UNO" button or draw 2 penalty cards
  • If you have no playable cards, you must draw a card from the draw pile
  • If the drawn card can be played, you may play it immediately

Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: AI makes basic moves and often misses strategic opportunities
  • Medium: AI plays strategically and occasionally calls out missed UNOs
  • Hard: AI plays optimally, times its special cards perfectly, and always catches missed UNOs
